# Sample Product Images for Testing

For testing the Freshfood CMS Template Generator, you can use product images from:

## Free Sample Images Sources:
1. Your own product photos
2. [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/product-packaging) - Search for "product packaging"
3. [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/search/product/) - Search for "grocery products"
4. Product images from your local grocery store (ensure you have rights to use them)

## Sample Product Types to Test:
- Beverage bottles (water, juice, soda)
- Snack packages (chips, cookies, crackers)
- Dairy products (milk, yogurt, cheese)
- Canned goods
- Cereal boxes
- Condiment bottles
- Frozen food packages

## Testing Tips:
1. Use images with clear, readable text
2. Ensure the product label is visible and well-lit
3. Test with various angles and lighting conditions
4. Try different product categories
5. Test batch processing with multiple images

## Expected Results:
The AI model should extract:
- Product name
- Brand name
- Category
- Price (if visible)
- Weight/Volume
- Ingredients (if visible on the label)
- Nutritional information (if visible)
- Barcode (if visible)
- Manufacturing/Expiry dates (if visible)
